How It Works

Z1 runs the cockpit. You choose the engine.

Z1 sits between your agency's approved LLM and your operations. Swap the model — the governance layer, memory, and audit trail stay in place. Your approved AI gets capabilities it was missing. Your oversight gets the accountability it requires.

Governance Layer

A fixed, defined capability set the AI cannot self-modify. Z1 determines what your approved agent can and cannot do — and those rules hold across IL2 through IL5. When the model changes, the rules don't.

Persistent Memory & Audit

Operational context, state, and history survive model changes. Every AI action is recorded in an HMAC-signed ledger — cryptographically auditable and defensible to oversight. Nothing disappears when an approval expires.

Model Agnostic by Design

New API feed, new model, new approval — put it in the pilot seat. Z1 takes over the system. Memory, governance, and audit trail carry over intact. CDAO changes the approved vendor list. Z1 doesn't care.
